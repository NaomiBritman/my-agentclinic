# Mission

Help adults keep up with exercise and nutrition, day by day, through a personal plan that gets gradually harder and stays within safe limits.

## Who it is for

Adults of any fitness level, from beginners to experienced exercisers. Each plan is scaled to the person's starting point.

## What the site does

- Shows a daily list of exercises to do.
- Lets the user mark whether they completed everything on that day's list.
- Tracks nutrition through manual logging.
- Raises exercise difficulty from week to week.
- Bases the plan on the user's weight and individual details.

## Current scope

- **Single local user:** there are no accounts or sign-in for now.
- **Manual nutrition logging:** the user enters what they ate. A food database may come later.
- **No nutrition targets yet:** logging can ship without safety caps, but it shows no targets, goals or judgments on intake. Personal targets wait until the nutrition caps are approved.

## Safety principles

These rules take priority over every other goal of the product.

1. **Limits are firm.** Weekly difficulty increases and nutrition targets have safety caps, and users cannot override them.
2. **Limits scale to the person.** Caps are set relative to the user's starting point and individual details, so they suit every fitness level.
3. **Cap values need approval.** Every cap value must come from reliable health sources approved by the project owner. No values are set yet, and none may be invented.
4. **Progress is gradual.** Difficulty rises in small steps, and no plan may skip ahead of its cap.
5. **No extreme dieting or overtraining.** The site must not encourage either one, whether through targets, wording, streaks or rewards.
6. **Not medical advice.** The site says this clearly and does not diagnose, treat or prescribe.
7. **Adults only.** The plan is designed for adults, so the site does not generate plans for anyone under 18.

## Success looks like

- A user can open the site and immediately see what to do today.
- Users can keep following their plan week after week as it gets harder.
- No generated plan or nutrition target ever goes past a safety cap.
