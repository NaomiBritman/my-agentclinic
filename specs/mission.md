# Mission

My Fit Plan helps adults reach a personal fitness goal, through an AI-built exercise plan and menu that stay within approved safety limits and are easy to follow day by day.

## Who it is for

Adults of any fitness level, from beginners to experienced exercisers, with a goal such as weight loss, toning or muscle building. Each plan is scaled to the person's starting point.

## What the site does

- Lets a person sign up, log in, and enter profile details such as age, height, weight and fitness level.
- Lets the person choose a goal, such as weight loss, toning or muscle building.
- Uses an AI agent to build a personal exercise plan and a menu from the profile and goal.
- Raises exercise difficulty from week to week, within the safety limits.
- Shows a daily list of exercises and planned meals to track.

## Daily tracking

- The person marks exercises as done and planned meals as eaten or not.
- The person can note anything else they ate. Notes are not counted or scored.
- Wording stays neutral, with no judgments on intake.
- Streaks and rewards never push anyone to eat less or train more.
- Food comparisons appear only against targets that fall within limits the project owner has approved.

## Current scope

- **Several local accounts:** each person has their own login and sees only their own data.
- **Local only:** the site runs on this computer and can't be reached from other devices.
- **No food database:** menus come from the AI agent, and off-menu food is a simple note.

## Safety principles

These rules take priority over every other goal of the product.

1. **Limits are firm.** Exercise plans, weekly difficulty increases, menus and nutrition targets all have safety limits, and neither users nor the AI agent can override them.
2. **Limits need approval.** Every limit value must come from reliable health sources approved by the project owner. No values are set yet, and none may be invented.
3. **No approved limits, no AI output.** People see no AI plan or menu until the limits covering it are approved.
4. **AI output is checked, not trusted.** The site checks every plan and menu against the limits in code, before anyone sees it. Output that fails is not shown.
5. **Goals don't override safety.** A goal never loosens a limit. If a request can't be met within the limits, the site doesn't produce a plan for it, says so neutrally, and suggests talking to a qualified health professional.
6. **Limits scale to the person.** Limits are set relative to the person's starting point and individual details, so they suit every fitness level.
7. **Progress is gradual.** Difficulty rises in small steps, and no plan may skip ahead of its limit.
8. **No extreme dieting or overtraining.** The site must not encourage either one, whether through plans, menus, targets, wording, streaks or rewards.
9. **Not medical advice.** The site says this clearly, says that plans and menus are AI-generated, and does not diagnose, treat or prescribe.
10. **Adults only.** The plan is designed for adults, so the site does not generate plans for anyone under 18.

## Success looks like

- A person can sign up, enter their details and goal, and get a personal plan and menu.
- A logged-in person can immediately see what to do and eat today, and track it.
- The site is easy to use on both phones and desktop computers.
- People can keep following their plan week after week as it gets harder.
- No plan, menu or target shown to anyone ever goes past an approved limit.
